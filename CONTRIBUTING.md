# Contributing

Issues, correction reports, public source links and pull requests are welcome. Identify the release, building/area, expected appearance and evidence date. Distinguish measured data from visual estimates; do not upload private people, credentials or restricted photos, plans, CAD/BIM or scans.

Only submit material you have permission to share. Identify its creator, source, licence and modifications. Contributions to the original-model layer must permit distribution under the Community License; independently licensed public data keeps its original licence. Contributions do not transfer ownership automatically or authorize commercial sublicensing beyond the contributor's actual grant.

Contributors may retain credit for their own additions. The original source **Manyousang Z / z-hstudio** remains credited. Do not relicense the entire model under conflicting terms or claim the whole reconstruction as independently original. No telemetry or executable anti-copy mechanism is accepted.
