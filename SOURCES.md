# Sources and their role

Sources are separated into included material/data and reference-only research. “Available online” and “packed in Blender” are not treated as redistribution permission.

- [OpenStreetMap contributors](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright): base layout and mapped features, ODbL 1.0. Project data was downloaded in September 2026 and is not promised current.
- [NSW 5 m elevation service](https://maps.six.nsw.gov.au/arcgis/rest/services/public/NSW_5M_Elevation/ImageServer): macro terrain, AHD evidence from catalog item 330. See [NSW copyright](https://www.spatial.nsw.gov.au/copyright). Five-metre terrain is not step/riser survey data.
- [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com/license), [ambientCG](https://docs.ambientcg.com/license/): approved generic surface maps and sky, CC0. Per-file source and SHA256 in [texture rights](docs/licensing/texture-rights.json).
- [University campus map](https://maps.sydney.edu.au/): identity/location checks; original cartography is not supplied.
- University heritage/archives, NSW public planning documents, architects' completed-project photographs and selected Wikimedia photographs: reference-only factual appearance evidence. Their photos and plans are not packaged as textures.

[Reference source index](docs/licensing/reference-source-index.json) retains source URLs and recorded licence descriptions while omitting local paths and downloaded documents. Some historical evidence is incomplete; it is not proof of current condition or a complete architectural-design rights grant.

[Rights review boundaries](docs/licensing/RIGHTS_REVIEW_REQUIRED.md) distinguish original modelling, factual references, imported models, licensed data and uncertain assets.
