# Public edition limitations

The public edition is a separately prepared publication copy. The private master, working head, source photos, original scans, checkpoints and rejected candidates remain privately preserved.

## REMOVED_FROM_PUBLIC_EDITION

- Three unregistered inspection scenes: graffiti mesh (Jimmy Gunawan, CC BY-NC-SA 4.0), graffiti point cloud (Jimmy Gunawan, CC BY 4.0), and the university's Tin Sheds model (redistribution permission unestablished).
- Their scene-exclusive geometry, point-cloud data, scan image textures and orphan datablocks. These assets were not registered into the main campus, so removal is not a deletion of a positioned campus landmark.
- Private reference photos, planning PDFs, raw download receipts, account data, working logs, embedded private notes and absolute host paths.
- Failed/unaccepted candidates and unrelated campus projects.

The campus geometry is not silently upgraded. Original uncertain heights, approximate footprints, simplified façades and known local defects remain. Numerical geometry checks do not certify survey accuracy, access, collisions or present-day as-built condition.

The public Blender copy retains the author's current scene materials and packed approved textures. The Lite GLB uses the historical export's lighter trees, texture resolution and reduced material graphs. Procedural roughness/bump and roof colours can differ visibly; Blender and GLB are not claimed visually identical. Historical snapshots retain their period's limitations.

Public history is reconstructed from retained files and contemporaneous reports. Old file timestamps are reported as local historical evidence, not independently verified publication timestamps. No Git commits or releases are backdated.
